# KPI Definitions

## 1. Total Revenue
Total billing amount generated from all patients.

Formula:
SUM(Billing Amount)

---

## 2. Average Patient Cost
Average healthcare cost incurred per patient.

Formula:
AVG(Billing Amount)

---

## 3. High-Risk Patient %
Percentage of patients whose billing amount falls in the top 10%.

Business Purpose:
Identifies costly patient segments for risk monitoring.

---

## 4. Diagnosis Cost Contribution
Total revenue contribution by each diagnosis category.

Business Purpose:
Helps identify expensive medical conditions.

---

## 5. Insurance Coverage Ratio
Percentage of patients covered by insurance.

Business Purpose:
Analyzes financial dependency on insurance systems.

---

## 6. Age Group Cost Analysis
Average billing amount across patient age groups.

Business Purpose:
Helps understand demographic healthcare trends.
