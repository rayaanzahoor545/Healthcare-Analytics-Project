# Stakeholder Requirements

## 1. Hospital Executives
Need visibility into:
- Total healthcare revenue
- High-cost diagnosis areas
- Patient risk trends

---

## 2. Finance Department
Need insights on:
- Billing performance
- Insurance vs self-pay analysis
- Revenue optimization

---

## 3. Operations Team
Need analytics for:
- Patient volume trends
- Resource allocation planning
- Demographic patterns

---

## 4. Insurance Analysts
Need to identify:
- High-risk patient groups
- Expensive treatment patterns
- Cost concentration trends
