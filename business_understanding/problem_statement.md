# Healthcare Analytics Business Problem

## Background
Healthcare organizations generate large volumes of patient, billing, and diagnosis-related data daily. However, without proper analytics systems, decision-makers struggle to identify high-cost patient segments, monitor diagnosis trends, and optimize financial performance.

## Business Problem
The healthcare organization wants to analyze patient demographics, medical conditions, and billing patterns to identify:

- High-cost patient groups
- Diagnosis categories driving healthcare expenses
- Financial performance trends
- Insurance coverage impact
- Operational risk indicators

## Objective
Develop an end-to-end Healthcare Analytics solution using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to support executive decision-making and improve healthcare cost visibility.

## Expected Outcomes
- Better financial visibility
- Early identification of high-risk patients
- Improved healthcare resource allocation
- Executive-level KPI reporting
