# Executive Summary

This project presents a comprehensive Healthcare Analytics solution developed to analyze patient demographics, diagnosis trends, billing behavior, and insurance-related financial performance.

Using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis identified high-cost patient segments, diagnosis categories contributing most to healthcare expenses, and demographic groups associated with elevated healthcare costs.

The dashboard and analytical outputs were designed to support executive-level healthcare decision-making, financial monitoring, and patient risk assessment.

Key areas analyzed include:
- Revenue performance
- Diagnosis cost analysis
- High-risk patient identification
- Insurance coverage trends
- Demographic healthcare patterns
