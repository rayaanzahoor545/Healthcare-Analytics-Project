# Key Business Insights

## 1. High-Cost Patients
A small percentage of patients contribute disproportionately to total healthcare revenue, indicating concentrated healthcare spending.

---

## 2. Diagnosis Trends
Certain medical conditions consistently generate significantly higher billing amounts compared to others.

---

## 3. Age-Based Cost Patterns
Senior patients show higher average healthcare costs, suggesting increased treatment complexity and healthcare utilization.

---

## 4. Insurance Impact
Patients with insurance coverage demonstrate different billing trends compared to self-pay patients.

---

## 5. Financial Risk Monitoring
Outlier billing cases indicate the need for improved healthcare cost monitoring systems.
