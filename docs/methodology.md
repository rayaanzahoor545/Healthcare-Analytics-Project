# Project Methodology

## Step 1: Business Understanding
Defined healthcare business objectives, KPIs, and stakeholder requirements.

---

## Step 2: Data Collection
Imported healthcare dataset from Kaggle into Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI environments.

---

## Step 3: Data Cleaning
Performed:
- Missing value handling
- Duplicate removal
- Data formatting
- Feature engineering

---

## Step 4: Exploratory Data Analysis
Analyzed:
- Billing distributions
- Demographic trends
- Diagnosis cost patterns
- Insurance impact

---

## Step 5: Dashboard Development
Built an executive-level Power BI dashboard for interactive reporting and business monitoring.

---

## Step 6: Insight Generation
Generated business recommendations based on analytical findings.
